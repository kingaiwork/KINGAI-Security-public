# KINGAI Security · Market & Partnership Direction

## English

KINGAI Security is positioned around a simple opportunity: security is becoming more complex while users still want an experience they can understand and trust.

### Who it can serve

- individuals who want simple everyday protection;
- creators and professionals protecting valuable work;
- small businesses that need safer endpoints without a heavy security stack;
- organizations seeking a future path to managed, policy-aware endpoint protection;
- device and platform partners exploring embedded security experiences.

### Commercial paths

Potential paths include free protection, premium consumer plans, small-business subscriptions, managed security services, enterprise endpoint offerings, OEM/embedded distribution and strategic security partnerships.

No revenue, customer-count or market-leadership claim is implied by these product directions.

### Why the product can matter strategically

KINGAI Security adds a trust layer to the wider KING AI ecosystem. As intelligent software becomes more capable, users and organizations need security that can understand more context while still keeping decisions explainable and controlled.

### Partnership interests

Relevant conversations may include endpoint-security technology, malware intelligence, device/OEM distribution, enterprise security, managed service providers, security research and long-term strategic investment.

# 中文

KINGAI Security 面向一个越来越明显的机会：安全威胁越来越复杂，但普通用户和企业仍然希望安全产品**简单、可信、看得懂**。

### 适用用户

- 希望获得日常安全保护的个人用户；
- 需要保护重要作品与数据的创作者、专业人士；
- 不希望部署沉重安全体系的小型企业；
- 希望未来获得统一终端安全管理能力的组织；
- 寻找设备/OEM/平台级安全合作的合作伙伴。

### 商业方向

未来可形成免费基础保护、消费者高级版、小企业订阅、托管安全服务、企业终端安全、OEM/嵌入式发行与战略安全合作等模式。

这些属于商业发展方向，不代表已经取得对应收入、客户规模或市场领先地位。

### 战略价值

随着智能软件能力越来越强，安全会成为 KING AI 整个生态的重要信任层。KINGAI Security 的长期价值在于：在理解更多上下文的同时，仍然让安全决策保持可解释、可控制、可恢复。

### 合作方向

欢迎终端安全、威胁情报、设备/OEM、企业安全、MSP、网络安全研究及长期战略资本等方向的合作交流。
