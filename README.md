## English

### Security should reduce uncertainty, not create more of it

Most people do not want to become security experts. They want to know whether their device is safe, what changed, whether something needs attention, and what to do next. KINGAI Security is being developed around that product experience: clearer protection, understandable findings, confident recovery and a path from personal security toward broader business trust.

### Why now

AI-enabled software, more connected devices, remote work and growing digital dependency increase the cost of poor security decisions. At the same time, users are tired of products that either hide everything or overwhelm them with alerts. The opportunity is a security experience that is strong enough to matter and simple enough to trust.

### The product promise

**Clear status** — Users should quickly understand whether attention is needed.

**Explainable findings** — A security finding should communicate what it means, not only that something was blocked.

**Recovery as a first-class experience** — Isolation, review and restore matter as much as initial detection.

**Privacy-aware intelligence** — Smarter protection should not require unnecessary exposure of sensitive data.

**Cross-platform direction** — The product can grow across supported personal and business environments as capabilities mature.

### Who it is for

- everyday users who want protection without security jargon;
- professionals and creators whose files and devices are central to their work;
- small businesses without large internal security teams;
- organizations seeking clearer endpoint visibility and policy over time;
- device, software and platform partners exploring embedded trust experiences.

### What success should feel like

A successful security product should create confidence without constant interruption. The user should feel that important risks are surfaced clearly, ordinary work is not unnecessarily blocked, and recovery is available when a decision needs to be reversed.

### Product journey

**Install / enable → understand current status → review meaningful findings → take a clear action → recover when appropriate → build long-term trust.**

### Long-term product direction

As the product matures, the broader opportunity can include richer reputation signals, behavior-aware intelligence, safer recommendations, small-business device visibility, managed protection and enterprise support. These are roadmap directions until publicly released and verified.

### Why it matters inside KING AI

Security becomes more important as AI systems gain more memory, automation and access to important workflows. KINGAI Security can become the trust layer beside KINGAI OS, Office, OPS and KINGAIBOT rather than existing as an isolated utility.

## 中文

### 安全软件应该减少不确定感，而不是制造更多恐慌

普通用户真正想知道的是：**现在安全吗？发生了什么？需要做什么？能不能恢复？** KINGAI Security 围绕这个体验设计，希望让安全状态更清楚、风险更容易理解、操作更有把握，并逐步从个人防护扩展到更广泛的企业安全与可信场景。

### 为什么是现在

AI 软件、联网设备、远程办公和数字资产不断增加，一次错误安全判断的成本越来越高；与此同时，用户也厌倦了“什么都不解释”或者“每天弹几十个警告”的安全产品。真正的机会是：保护能力足够强，同时体验足够清楚。

### 产品承诺

**状态清楚** —— 一眼知道是否需要处理。  
**风险可理解** —— 告诉用户“意味着什么”，而不是只报一个警告。  
**恢复完整** —— 隔离、复查、恢复都属于核心体验。  
**隐私意识** —— 更聪明不等于无边界上传数据。  
**跨平台方向** —— 随产品成熟覆盖更多个人与企业环境。

### 适合谁

个人用户、专业人士、创作者、小型企业、未来企业终端安全场景，以及希望探索嵌入式安全体验的设备与软件合作伙伴。

### 理想使用路径

**开启保护 → 看懂状态 → 处理真正重要的问题 → 必要时恢复 → 长期建立信任。**

### 与 KING AI 的关系

OS 是计算环境，Office 是高频工作，OPS 是基础设施，BOT 是受控行动，而 Security 负责提供更清楚的可信与保护入口。
